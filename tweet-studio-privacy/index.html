Tweet Studio 隐私政策与支持 / Privacy & Support

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-11-11
开发者 / Developer: 谷粒（kuhung）
支持邮箱 / Support: support@kuhung.me

简体中文正文

Tweet Studio 帮助你把在 X（Twitter）网页中选择的推文制作成图片。以下说明适用于本扩展对数据的处理。

当你点击推文旁的 Tweet Studio 按钮时，扩展会读取所选推文及可见引用中的作者显示名称、用户名、认证状态、头像地址、正文、时间、来源链接、图片地址及可读取的互动字段，用于在编辑器中生成图片。按钮开启时，内容脚本会检查X页面的推文元素以插入入口；用于编辑器的推文记录在你点击入口后创建。

这些记录和你的样式参数、按钮显示偏好存储在当前浏览器的本地扩展存储中。扩展最多保留最近20条捕获记录；新增记录时淘汰更早记录。没有基于天数的自动清理期限。样式和显示偏好会保留供下次使用。你可以通过清除本扩展的数据或卸载扩展移除本地保存的内容。你主动下载或复制的图片由你自行管理，卸载扩展不会删除已经导出的文件或剪贴板内容。

图片在你的浏览器中生成。为绘制头像和配图，扩展会通过HTTPS向X的媒体服务pbs.twimg.com请求对应图片。该服务会收到获取资源所需的正常网络请求信息，其处理由其自身政策约束。扩展没有向开发者发送推文内容的接口，没有广告、使用统计或追踪代码，不读取你的X密码、认证令牌或浏览器历史记录。

扩展不出售数据，也不将捕获的内容用于广告画像或与推文图片制作无关的用途。你选择分享或上传导出的图片时，由你使用的分享目标处理相应图片；扩展本身不自动发布推文或向他人发送图片。

如有问题，请通过公开支持邮箱 support@kuhung.me 联系开发者。

English text

Tweet Studio helps you make images from posts you select on X (Twitter). This notice describes data handling by the extension.

When you click the Tweet Studio button beside a post, the extension reads the selected post and visible quoted content, including the author’s display name and handle, verification status, avatar URL, text, date, source URL, image URLs and available engagement fields. These fields are used to render the image in the editor. While the button is enabled, a content script checks post elements on X to add the entry button. A captured record for the editor is created after you click it.

Captured records, design settings and button visibility preferences are stored in the current browser’s local extension storage. The extension keeps up to 20 recent captures, removing older records as new captures are added. Records are not automatically removed after a fixed number of days. Preferences remain available for later use. You can remove stored data by clearing the extension’s data or uninstalling the extension. Downloaded files and copied images are managed by you; uninstalling does not remove previously exported files or clipboard content.

Images are rendered in your browser. To draw avatars and attached images, the extension makes HTTPS image requests to X’s media service at pbs.twimg.com. That service receives normal network information needed to serve those resources and handles it under its own policies. The extension has no endpoint that sends captured posts to the developer, and contains no advertising, usage analytics or tracking code. It does not read your X password, authentication tokens or browser history.

The extension does not sell data or use captured content for advertising profiles or purposes unrelated to making post images. If you choose to share an exported image, your chosen destination handles it. The extension does not automatically publish posts or send images to other people.

For questions, contact the developer at support@kuhung.me.
